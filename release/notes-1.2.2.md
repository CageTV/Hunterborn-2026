Hunterborn 2026 1.2.2. Install over 1.2.1. Needs the original Hunterborn (Nexus 7900), SkyUI, SKSE and SKSE Menu Framework 3.

Fixed in 1.2.2:
- Hunterborn's own menus could not be brought back. "How a click on a kill works" rewrote the four "Click on ..." settings on every game load, so choosing Prompt on the Tweaks page was undone at the next load, and choosing "Hunterborn's menus" on the Extras page left the stored one-click value in place. Now changing one of those four settings by hand switches the mode to Hunterborn's menus, so your choice is kept, and choosing Hunterborn's menus puts the four back to the original prompts.
