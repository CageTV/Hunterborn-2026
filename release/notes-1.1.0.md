Hunterborn 2026 1.1.0. Install over 1.0.0 (or the original Hunterborn SE, then this). Needs the original Hunterborn (Nexus 7900), SkyUI, SKSE and SKSE Menu Framework 3.

New in 1.1.0:
- One click hunting (Extras page, "How a click on a kill works"). Clicking a fresh kill field dresses it and processes it (skin, harvest, butcher) in one go; without a hunting knife you get less, better knives give better quality. Sneak-click picks the carcass up. The old prompts and a Simple Hunting Overhaul loot-window mode are still there. New fresh-kill action "Field dress and process" on the Tweaks page.
- Hunterborn Animations integration (optional in the installer, needs Hunterborn - Animations, Nexus 80677, installed first; none of its files are included): kneel, dress cut, kneel idle, stab-and-pull, stand-up in one sequence. With bare hands the claw dress and the harvest-fail pull play. Carrying poses follow the animal's size (bears across the back, wolves and deer left or right, foxes on the hip, hares and chickens into the inventory). Hunting in Skyrim's "Skinning Quest Started" box is removed.
- Vols Cloak PBR (optional, Community Shaders only): PBR textures and PGPatcher json for the Vols cloaks. Needs Hunterborn - Animations for the mesh and base textures.
- Animallica lore filter (Extras page): "Lore friendly" or "Lore friendly (stricter)" takes the creatures Animallica's lore editions remove out of every spawn list at run time, on the one Animallica plugin; neither lore plugin is needed.
- Fixed: with Simple Hunterborn installed Hunterborn showed as running without ever being started, so the automatic start skipped and the first carcass asked "start Hunterborn now?". It now starts properly.
- The DLL (new and older builds) is 1.1.0.
